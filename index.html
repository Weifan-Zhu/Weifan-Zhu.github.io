03 / PRODUCTION AI PROJECT

Personal project · AI-assisted development

Literature
AI Assistant

I designed and operate an unattended literature intelligence platform for condensed-matter researchers. It tracks new arXiv papers, filters them with domain-specific physics keywords, parses PDFs and figures, routes multimodal analysis across several LLMs, and turns the results into a searchable collaboration workspace.

  • Tracks cond-mat.str-el and cond-mat.mtrl-sci with about 50 weighted physics keywords; a resilient HTML crawler maintains over 99% production fetch success.
  • Combines DeepSeek structured summaries, Gemini multimodal analysis, and Kimi vision for figure interpretation and paper-level Q&A.
  • Flask, Jinja2, and SQLite power authentication, search, collections, tags, uploads, reading history, and BibTeX / RIS export.
  • Production corpus: 358 papers, 3,814 figures, 358 AI summaries, and 13 registered users, supported by scheduling, retries, monitoring, and backups.
  • Resolved caption-versus-citation parsing errors with layered filters and validated the repair on a 142-PDF regression set with zero errors.
