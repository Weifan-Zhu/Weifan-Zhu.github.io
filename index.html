03 / AI SYSTEM IN RESEARCH USE

Personal project · Project lead & system architect · AI-assisted development

Literature
AI Assistant

As project lead and system architect, I designed an unattended literature platform for condensed-matter physics. It turns new arXiv papers into searchable, figure-aware research notes and a shared web workspace. I lead the requirements, architecture, algorithm design, validation, deployment, and operations; coding is AI-assisted.

  • The daily pipeline follows cond-mat.str-el and cond-mat.mtrl-sci, screens papers with about 50 weighted physics keywords, downloads PDFs, parses text and figures, and publishes research notes; a custom arXiv HTML crawler maintains over 99% fetch success.
  • DeepSeek produces structured summaries, Gemini handles detailed multimodal analysis, and Kimi vision interprets figures; users can also ask questions about individual papers.
  • Python, Flask, Jinja2, PyMuPDF, and a 13-table SQLite database power search, accounts, collections, tags, custom uploads, reading history, and BibTeX / RIS export.
  • By August 2026, the system had processed 358 papers, extracted 3,814 figures, and generated 358 AI summaries for 13 registered users, with scheduling, retries, fallback paths, single-instance locking, monitoring, and automatic backups.
  • Layered caption filters reached zero misclassifications on a 142-PDF regression set; the repair workflow fixed 544 corrupted records and removed 820 orphaned image files.
